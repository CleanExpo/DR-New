# SEO Optimization Implementation Summary

## Overview

Complete SEO optimization strategy and code implementations to address BrightLocal audit findings for disasterrecovery.com.au (315+ pages, 4.7-star rating, 49 Google reviews).

---

## Audit Findings Addressed

Issue | Current | Problem | Solution
------|---------|---------|----------
Open Graph Duplicates | 27 pages (35%) | Generic tags across location pages | Unique OG per page using generateLocationSEO()
Twitter Card Duplicates | 66 pages (85.71%) | Identical descriptions | Unique Twitter descriptions per page type
Content Under 500 Words | 46 pages | Thin content | 700-1,500 word templates by page type
Schema Markup | Present but inconsistent | Missing HowTo, AggregateRating, breadcrumbs | Comprehensive schema generators added
Internal Linking | Basic structure | No silo strategy | 3-silo approach (Services, Locations, Insurance)
Featured Snippets | Minimal captures | Content not optimized | HowTo schema + list/table formatting
