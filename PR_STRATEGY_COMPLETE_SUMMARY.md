## PITFALL AVOIDANCE

### DON'T:
- ❌ Mass email blast (personalize each pitch to journalist)
- ❌ Pitch wrong journalist for topic (research their beat first)
- ❌ Embellish or inflate data (journalists fact-check, credibility is everything)
- ❌ Be pushy or demanding (journalists get 100+ pitches daily)
- ❌ Miss deadlines (if journalist needs quote by 2pm, deliver by 1:30pm)
- ❌ Burn bridges (even bad interactions can become future opportunities)
- ❌ Forget to log interactions (tracking is how you improve)

### DO:
- ✅ Personalize every pitch (mention their recent work if possible)
- ✅ Provide real value (data, quotes, case studies journalists need)
- ✅ Respond fast (2-hour response time builds reputation)
- ✅ Be accurate (verify all data before sharing)
- ✅ Thank journalists (appreciation builds relationships)
- ✅ Follow up appropriately (7 days, 14 days, not daily)
- ✅ Track everything (learn from what works and what doesn't)

---

## CRISIS MANAGEMENT

### If Misquoted or Factual Error:
1. **Stay professional** (journalist relationship is long-term)
2. **Politely request correction** with evidence
3. **Offer clarification interview** if needed
4. **Don't burn bridges** over minor errors
5. **Use template** from EMAIL_SWIPE_FILE.md (SPECIAL S2)

### If Negative Coverage:
1. **Assess if response needed** (sometimes silence is better)
2. **Prepare factual, non-defensive response** if required
3. **Offer follow-up interview** for balanced coverage
4. **Learn and adjust** future pitches to avoid similar issues

### If Journalist Complains:
1. **Apologize immediately**
2. **Ask for feedback** on what to improve
3. **Remove from pitch list** if requested
4. **Note preferences** in database for future reference
