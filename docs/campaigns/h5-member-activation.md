# NRPG member activation — Australian H5 readiness

## Purpose

Activate NRPG members around calm, evidence-based H5 readiness without creating panic or making unsupported product claims.

## Member message

Australia has confirmed H5 bird flu in migratory seabirds. The Australian Government states there is no evidence of mass mortality, no evidence of infection in poultry or the wider agriculture industry, and the current risk to human health remains low.

Members should repeat the official message and avoid alarmist language.

## Member actions

1. Complete the CARSI awareness pathway.
2. Review the public reporting pathway: 1800 675 888.
3. Prepare PPE and clean/dirty zone documentation templates.
4. Use RestoreAssist.app for site records.
5. Do not handle wildlife without authorisation.
6. Do not claim a property is disease-free.
7. Do not claim a product or dry-fogging method is required by government unless written authority direction and product evidence supports the claim.
