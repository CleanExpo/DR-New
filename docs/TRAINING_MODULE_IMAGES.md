## Image Quality Standards

### Technical Requirements:
- **Resolution:** 2K (1920x1080 or 2048x1080)
- **Format:** JPG
- **Quality:** Professional photographic quality
- **File Size:** 200-800 KB per image
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 4:3 depending on content

### Content Requirements:
- ✅ Photorealistic (not illustrated/CGI unless specifically an infographic)
- ✅ Professional composition and lighting
- ✅ Australian context where relevant
- ✅ IICRC-appropriate equipment and procedures
- ✅ Privacy-safe (no identifiable faces)
- ✅ Licensing-safe (no text, logos, watermarks, copyrighted elements)
- ✅ Clean and professional aesthetic
- ✅ Appropriate for emergency services/professional context

### Prohibited Elements:
- ❌ People's faces visible
- ❌ Brand names, logos, or trademarks
- ❌ Real company names or addresses
- ❌ Actual personal information
- ❌ Horror or extreme damage aesthetic
- ❌ Unsafe practices or violations
- ❌ Low quality or amateur photography
