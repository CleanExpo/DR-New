## 3. Trading Names and Their Purpose

### Disaster Recovery
The primary consumer brand. Property owners visit the platform to find, compare, and engage qualified restoration contractors for work including flood damage, fire damage, storm damage, mould remediation, and general property restoration. The platform facilitates the matching process between property owners and contractors.

**Key distinction:** Contractors are independent operators. The platform facilitates matching only; it does not employ contractors or take responsibility for their work.

### NRPG (National Restoration Partners Guild)
The professional body for restoration contractors, operated by Disaster Recovery Pty Ltd. NRPG provides:
- Professional certification and verification of contractors
- Training modules and continuing professional development (delivered via CARSI)
- Industry standards and best practices
- A professional network and community
- Contractor profile listings on the Disaster Recovery marketplace

### Restore Assist
A service arm that helps property owners navigate insurance claims related to property damage. This service assists homeowners with:
- Understanding their insurance policy coverage
- Documenting damage for claims purposes
- Navigating the claims process
- Connecting with appropriate contractors through the Disaster Recovery platform

## 6. Platform / SaaS Model Explanation

The technology platform is a **Next.js 14 web application** deployed as a SaaS (Software as a Service) product. Key technical components:

- **Frontend:** TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Next.js API routes, Prisma ORM
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (hosted on Supabase)
- **Payments:** Stripe integration for subscription billing and commission processing
- **Hosting:** Vercel (cloud deployment)

### How the Marketplace Works
1. Property owners submit a job request describing their damage/restoration needs
2. The platform matches the request with verified NRPG-certified contractors
3. Contractors submit quotes through the platform
4. The property owner selects a contractor
5. The platform facilitates the engagement and takes a commission on the matched job
6. The contractor invoices the client directly; the platform commission is a separate charge

### SaaS Subscription Model
Contractors pay recurring subscription fees for:
- Profile listing on the marketplace
- Access to job leads in their service area
- NRPG certification and training resources
- Business tools and analytics dashboard
