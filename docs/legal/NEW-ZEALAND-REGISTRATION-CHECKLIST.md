## 5. GST Registration (NZ)

**Portal:** https://www.ird.govt.nz

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | Free |
| **Mandatory if** | Taxable supplies exceed or are expected to exceed NZD $60,000 in any 12-month period |
| **Rate** | 15% (standard rate) |
| **Reporting** | Monthly, 2-monthly, or 6-monthly |

### Requirements
- [ ] NZ IRD number
- [ ] Estimate of NZ taxable turnover
- [ ] Choose accounting basis (invoice or payments) with accountant
- [ ] Choose filing frequency with accountant

### Steps
- [ ] Register for GST via myIR (Inland Revenue online portal)
- [ ] Set up GST return filing schedule
- [ ] Configure Stripe to charge NZ GST where applicable
- [ ] Review cross-border GST rules (AU entity supplying digital services to NZ customers)

### Cross-Border GST Considerations
- [ ] An Australian entity supplying digital services to NZ consumers may need to register for NZ GST even without a physical NZ presence
- [ ] Review the NZ "marketplace rules" for GST on digital services
- [ ] Discuss double-taxation avoidance with accountant (AU-NZ DTA)

## 6. Employer Obligations (if hiring NZ staff)

### Minimum Requirements
- [ ] Written employment agreements for all employees (mandatory in NZ)
- [ ] Comply with the Employment Relations Act 2000
- [ ] Minimum wage compliance (check current NZ minimum wage)
- [ ] KiwiSaver employer contributions (3% minimum of gross salary)
- [ ] PAYE deductions and filing with IRD
- [ ] Holiday Act 2003 compliance (4 weeks annual leave, public holidays, sick leave)
- [ ] Health and safety obligations under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015
- [ ] ACC (Accident Compensation Corporation) levies (automatic, deducted via PAYE)

### Steps
- [ ] Set up as an employer with IRD
- [ ] Establish payroll compliant with NZ requirements
- [ ] Prepare compliant employment agreement templates
- [ ] Register for KiwiSaver employer contributions

## 7. NZ Privacy Act 2020 Obligations

The NZ Privacy Act 2020 applies to any entity that carries on business in NZ or collects personal information about NZ individuals.

### Requirements
- [ ] Appoint a Privacy Officer (can be an existing staff member)
- [ ] Publish a NZ-compliant Privacy Policy (or update existing AU policy to cover NZ)
- [ ] Comply with the 13 Information Privacy Principles (IPPs)
- [ ] Implement processes for responding to privacy requests
- [ ] Mandatory breach notification to the NZ Privacy Commissioner for "notifiable" breaches
- [ ] Review data storage and cross-border transfer arrangements (data may be stored in AU)

### Key Differences from Australian Privacy Law
| Area | Australia | New Zealand |
|---|---|---|
| **Legislation** | Privacy Act 1988 | Privacy Act 2020 |
| **Principles** | 13 APPs | 13 IPPs |
| **Breach notification** | Mandatory (notifiable breaches) | Mandatory (notifiable breaches) |
| **Threshold** | $3M turnover (with exceptions) | No turnover threshold; applies to all agencies |
| **Regulator** | OAIC | NZ Privacy Commissioner |

## 8. Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 (NZ)

The Consumer Guarantees Act (CGA) applies to services supplied to consumers in NZ.

### Obligations
- [ ] Services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill
- [ ] Services must be fit for the purpose made known by the consumer
- [ ] Services must be completed within a reasonable time
- [ ] Platform terms and conditions must not contract out of CGA protections for consumers
- [ ] Review the terms of service for NZ compliance

### Steps
- [ ] Review platform terms of service for CGA compliance
- [ ] Ensure refund/cancellation policies comply with NZ consumer law
- [ ] Note: The CGA cannot be contracted out of for consumer transactions

### Marks to Register
- [ ] "Disaster Recovery" (word mark and/or logo)
- [ ] "NRPG" / "National Restoration Partners Guild" (word mark and/or logo)
- [ ] "Restore Assist" (word mark and/or logo)

### Steps
- [ ] Search existing NZ trademarks at https://www.iponz.govt.nz
- [ ] File trademark applications for each mark
- [ ] Consider filing in relevant classes (e.g., Class 35 - business services, Class 37 - construction/restoration, Class 42 - technology/SaaS)
- [ ] Monitor for any oppositions during the examination period
