## 12. Technical Implementation Details

### Alt Text Formula Used:
```
[Service] [Location] by [Credential] [Professional Name] - [Key Features] - [Coverage Area]
```

**Example:**
"Emergency water damage restoration Brisbane by IICRC Master Restorer Phill McGurk - 60-minute response for burst pipes floods storm water damage - industrial water extraction structural drying Brisbane Ipswich Logan"

### Title Attribute Formula:
```
[Service] [Location] | [Credential/Feature] | [Phone CTA]
```

**Example:**
"Emergency Water Damage Restoration Brisbane | Master Restorer 60-Min Response | 1300 309 361"

### Loading Strategy Logic:
- **Priority:** `<Image priority>` - Hero images, first image in viewport
- **Lazy:** `loading="lazy"` - All images below fold, secondary images

### Structured Data Schema Types:
- **Organization** - Logo, business details
- **ImageObject** - Individual images
- **HowTo** - Before/after restoration processes
