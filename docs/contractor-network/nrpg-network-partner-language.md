# NRPG Network Partner Description Language

**Document type:** Brand and compliance language standard
**Applies to:** Website copy, contractor profiles, directory listings, marketing materials, partner communications
**Last updated:** 2026-04-09
**Related:** DR-460

---

## Purpose

This document standardises how NRPG and its contractor network are described across all customer-facing and partner-facing touchpoints. All copywriters, contractors, and third-party publishers must follow this language standard.

The non-negotiable principle: **NRPG is a restoration network. It is not a claims advocate, insurance broker, or legal representative.**

---

## 1. Approved Description Variants

### Short (under 30 words) — for directory listings, map profiles, taglines

> NRPG is a national network of IICRC-certified restoration contractors, providing water, fire, and storm damage restoration services with full documentation support across Australia and New Zealand.

> Australia and New Zealand's network of independently certified restoration specialists — coordinating professional damage assessment, remediation, and insurer correspondence for residential and commercial clients.

### Medium (60–100 words) — for contractor profile bios, Google Business Profile descriptions, trade directory entries

> NRPG (National Restoration Partner Group) is a vetted network of independently licensed and IICRC-certified restoration contractors operating across Australia and New Zealand. Each network partner holds their own trade licences, insurance, and professional certifications. NRPG coordinates restoration project management, full scope-of-works documentation, and insurer correspondence on behalf of clients — ensuring restoration work meets industry standards and that claims are supported by accurate, professional documentation.

### Long (150–250 words) — for About pages, marketing collateral, partner onboarding materials

> NRPG (National Restoration Partner Group) connects property owners and managers with a vetted network of independently licensed and IICRC-certified restoration contractors across Australia and New Zealand.

> Every contractor in the NRPG network independently holds the licences, insurance policies, and IICRC certifications required to operate in their trade category and jurisdiction. NRPG does not employ these contractors directly — each operates as an independent business that has met NRPG's vetting criteria and ongoing compliance requirements.

> When a property is damaged by water, fire, storm, or mould, NRPG coordinates the full restoration project: engaging the appropriate network contractor, preparing scope-of-works documentation, and managing correspondence with the property owner's insurer on their behalf. NRPG ensures that the work carried out, and the documentation provided to the insurer, accurately reflects industry-standard restoration practices.

> NRPG does not act as an insurance broker, manage insurance policies, provide legal advice, or guarantee any insurance claim outcome. The network's role is to ensure professional restoration is completed to the required standard and that the documentation supporting a claim is accurate, complete, and properly submitted.

## 2. Approved Service Language

### What NRPG does

Use these descriptions accurately and only where they apply:

- **Restoration work:** Water damage restoration, fire and smoke remediation, storm and cyclone damage repair, mould remediation, structural drying
- **Claim documentation:** Prepares scope-of-works reports, photo documentation packs, moisture mapping, and drying logs to support an insurance claim
- **Insurer correspondence coordination:** Manages written and phone correspondence with the property owner's insurer on the client's behalf, ensuring restoration scope and costs are accurately communicated
- **Project management:** Coordinates contractor scheduling, site access, subcontractor engagement, and completion sign-off
- **Compliance assurance:** Ensures all work is completed to IICRC and Australian Standards, with auditable records

### What NRPG does NOT do

Never describe NRPG as doing any of the following:

- Acting as an insurance broker or intermediary in the legal/financial sense
- Negotiating insurance settlements or policy entitlements
- Guaranteeing that a claim will be approved, paid, or paid in full
- Providing legal advice to clients
- Representing clients in disputes with insurers in a legal capacity
- Holding or managing insurance policies on behalf of clients

## 3. IICRC Certification Language

### Approved

> All contractors in the NRPG network independently hold IICRC certification relevant to their trade category.

> NRPG network partners are independently IICRC-certified.

> Work is carried out by IICRC-certified contractors who independently maintain their own certification and continuing education requirements.

> NRPG's contractor vetting process requires current IICRC certification as a condition of network membership.

### Not approved

Do not use any of the following:

- "NRPG is IICRC certified" (NRPG as a company does not hold IICRC certification — individual contractors do)
- "IICRC-certified company"
- "We are certified to [specific IICRC standard]" (unless referring to a specific individual contractor's credential)
- Any claim implying NRPG itself holds or has been awarded an IICRC credential

### Why this matters

IICRC certifications are held by individuals and firms, not by networks or referral groups. Claiming NRPG is IICRC certified when it is not would be a misleading representation under ACL s18. The correct framing always places certification with the individual contractor, not the network body.

## 4. Prohibited Phrases

The following phrases must never appear in any NRPG or DR-branded content:

| Prohibited phrase | Reason |
|---|---|
| "We fight your insurer" | Implies adversarial legal representation — not permitted |
| "We maximise your payout" | Unverifiable outcome claim, potential ACL s18 breach |
| "Claims advocate" | Implies a licensed or quasi-legal advocacy role NRPG does not hold |
| "Guarantee your claim is approved" | Unverifiable and false — NRPG has no control over claim decisions |
| "We get you the best settlement" | Implies financial negotiation role — not our service |
| "Insurance claim specialists" | Ambiguous — implies financial/legal claims handling, not restoration |
| "Faster claim payouts" | Unverifiable outcome claim |
| "We ensure your insurer pays" | Misleading — insurer decisions are outside NRPG's control |
| "Claims management" | Regulated term in some contexts; avoid unless legal review confirms applicability |
| "NRPG is IICRC certified" | Factually incorrect — contractors hold IICRC certs, not the network |
| "We act on your behalf with your insurer" (unqualified) | Acceptable only if qualified with "for restoration correspondence" — not as a legal representative |

## 6. ACL Section 18 Compliance Notes

All NRPG and DR content must comply with the Australian Consumer Law (ACL) s18, which prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct in trade or commerce.

### Key obligations

**No unverifiable statistics.**
Do not use claims such as "95% of our claims are approved" or "average payout 40% higher" unless these figures are documented, auditable, and can be substantiated if challenged by the ACCC or a court.

**No misleading claims about outcomes.**
NRPG cannot guarantee, imply, or suggest that using its services will result in a higher, faster, or more favourable insurance outcome. Insurer decisions are made by the insurer. NRPG's role is accurate documentation and professional restoration — not outcome determination.

**Accurate representation of services.**
Any claim about what NRPG does must reflect what it actually does. Do not describe NRPG as a "claims manager", "insurance advocate", or similar unless the service offering genuinely includes regulated claims management and appropriate licensing has been obtained.

**Testimonials and reviews.**
Testimonials must be genuine, attributable, and not selectively presented to create a false overall impression. Do not publish edited or paraphrased testimonials without the author's approval.

**Comparative claims.**
Do not make comparative claims (e.g., "the best restoration network in Australia") without a documented, verifiable basis.

### Review trigger

Any new landing page, directory listing, or marketing asset that includes outcome language, statistics, or comparative claims must be reviewed against this document before publication.
