## Part 2: Job Workflow

### Step 1: Job Notification

When a client submits a service request in your service area:

1. **Notification Received:** Push notification + SMS + Email
2. **Job Details:** Location, service type, urgency level, estimated value
3. **Response Window:** 15 minutes for emergency, 2 hours for standard
4. **Acceptance:** Click "Accept Job" in dashboard

**Example - Sydney Metro Emergency:**
> **Job Alert:** Water damage - Burst pipe
> **Location:** Parramatta, NSW 2150
> **Urgency:** Emergency (Response required within 15 min)
> **Estimated Value:** $3,500 - $5,000
> **Insurance:** Allianz Australia (Claim #AL-2024-089234)

### Step 2: Site Assessment

**Pre-Visit Checklist:**
- [ ] Review client notes and photos
- [ ] Check equipment requirements
- [ ] Verify insurance coverage
- [ ] Confirm access arrangements

**Clean Claims Documentation:**
1. Open Clean Claims mobile app
2. Create new assessment
3. Document with photos (minimum 12 required)
4. Record moisture readings
5. Generate preliminary scope

**Required Photos:**
- Wide shot of affected area
- Close-up of damage
- Water source (if visible)
- Adjacent unaffected areas
- Building exterior
- Any safety hazards

### Step 3: Quote Submission

**Quote Components:**
1. **Labour:** Hourly rates × estimated hours
2. **Equipment:** Dehumidifiers, air movers, extractors
3. **Materials:** Cleaning agents, protective equipment
4. **Disposal:** Waste removal costs
5. **GST:** 10% on all taxable items

**Example Quote Structure:**
```
Water Damage Restoration - Parramatta, NSW
Labour (16 hours @ $95/hr):        $1,520.00
Equipment (5-day rental):            $890.00
Materials:                           $245.00
Disposal:                            $180.00
Subtotal:                          $2,835.00
GST (10%):                           $283.50
TOTAL:                             $3,118.50
```

### Step 4: Work Authorization

**Client Approval:**
- Quote sent via platform
- Client reviews and approves
- Work order automatically generated
- Insurance notification (if applicable)

**Insurance Coordination:**
- Platform notifies insurer
- Adjuster may request additional documentation
- Approval typically within 24-48 hours
- Work proceeds upon authorization

### Step 5: Project Execution

**Daily Updates Required:**
- Progress photos (minimum 6 per day)
- Moisture reading logs
- Equipment placement documentation
- Any scope changes

**Client Communication:**
- Morning arrival notification
- End-of-day summary
- Milestone updates
- Completion notification

### Step 6: Completion & Payment

**Final Documentation:**
- Before/after photo comparison
- Moisture clearance readings
- Certificate of completion
- Client sign-off

**Payment Processing:**
- Invoice automatically generated
- Client payment (if direct)
- Insurance payment (if claim)
- Funds released to contractor (less platform fee)
- Typical payment timeline: 3-5 business days

## Part 3: Payment Processing

### Understanding the Payment Flow

```
Client/Insurer Payment
        ↓
DR-NRPG Escrow Account
        ↓
[Verification Period: 48 hours]
        ↓
Contractor Payout (less 8% platform fee)
```

### Platform Fee Structure

| Service Type | Platform Fee | GST |
|--------------|--------------|-----|
| Standard Jobs | 8% | 10% on fee |
| Emergency Response | 10% | 10% on fee |
| Insurance Claims | 6% | 10% on fee |

**Example Calculation:**
```
Job Value: $5,000.00
Platform Fee (8%): $400.00
GST on Fee: $40.00
Contractor Receives: $4,560.00
```

### Payment Schedule

**Direct Client Payments:**
- Immediate upon job completion
- 48-hour verification period
- Released to contractor account

**Insurance Claim Payments:**
- Upon insurer approval
- May require additional documentation
- Typical timeline: 5-10 business days

**Payment Methods:**
- Direct bank transfer (preferred)
- PayPal
- Stripe Connect

## Part 4: Clean Claims Integration

### What is Clean Claims?

Clean Claims is the mandatory documentation platform for all DR-NRPG contractors. It provides:

- Real-time job site documentation
- Insurance-compliant reporting
- Photo management and storage
- Progress tracking
- Quality assurance tools

### Clean Claims Workflow

**1. Job Setup:**
```
Login → Select Job → Create Assessment
```

**2. Documentation:**
- Take photos directly in app
- Add voice notes
- Record measurements
- Document moisture readings

**3. Reporting:**
- Generate assessment report
- Submit to insurance (if applicable)
- Share with client
- Store for compliance

### Required Clean Claims Certification

All DR-NRPG contractors must complete:
- Clean Claims Fundamentals (2 hours)
- Mobile App Training (1 hour)
- Insurance Reporting Standards (1 hour)

**Certification Renewal:** Annually

## Part 5: Communication Standards

### Client Communication

**Response Time Requirements:**
- Emergency: Within 15 minutes
- Urgent: Within 2 hours
- Standard: Within 24 hours

**Communication Channels:**
1. **Platform Messaging:** Primary method
2. **SMS:** For urgent updates
3. **Email:** For formal documentation
4. **Phone:** Only when requested

**Professional Standards:**
- Use Australian English spelling
- Address clients by preferred name
- Provide clear, jargon-free explanations
- Document all communications in platform

### Insurance Communication

**Key Contacts:**
- **Claim Number:** Always reference in communications
- **Adjuster:** Primary insurance contact
- **DR-NRPG Support:** For platform issues

**Required Documentation:**
- Initial assessment report
- Progress photos
- Change orders
- Final completion report
