## Part 1: Understanding CEC Requirements

### What are Continual Education Credits?

Continual Education Credits (CECs) are professional development points required to maintain your IICRC certification and DR-NRPG contractor status. They ensure you stay current with:

- Industry best practices
- New technologies and equipment
- Regulatory changes
- Safety protocols
- Australian standards updates

### IICRC Australia CEC Requirements

**Certification Renewal Cycle:** 3 years

| Certification Level | CECs Required | Minimum per Year |
|---------------------|---------------|------------------|
| IICRC Certified Technician | 14 CECs | 4 CECs |
| IICRC Master Technician | 14 CECs | 4 CECs |
| IICRC Advanced Master | 14 CECs | 4 CECs |

**Grace Period:** 6 months after expiry (with late fees)

### DR-NRPG Additional Requirements

Beyond IICRC requirements, DR-NRPG contractors must complete:

- **Platform Training Updates:** 4 CECs annually
- **Australian Standards Updates:** 2 CECs annually
- **Safety Refresher:** 2 CECs annually

**Total DR-NRPG Requirement:** 8 CECs per year

## Part 2: CARSI CEC Tracking System

### What is CARSI?

The **Cleaning And Restoration Services Institute (CARSI)** is the Australian body responsible for:

- CEC program administration
- Training provider accreditation
- CEC tracking and verification
- Certification renewal processing

**Website:** [carsi.com.au](https://carsi.com.au)  
**Contact:** 1300 CARSI AUS (1300 227 742)

### Accessing Your CEC Dashboard

**Login Process:**
1. Visit [carsi.com.au/contractors](https://carsi.com.au/contractors)
2. Click "Contractor Login"
3. Enter your IICRC ID
4. Verify with SMS code
5. Access your CEC dashboard

**Dashboard Features:**
- Current CEC balance
- Certification expiry dates
- Training history
- Upcoming courses
- Renewal reminders

### CEC Categories

**Category A: Technical Training (Maximum 10 CECs per cycle)**
- IICRC-approved courses
- Manufacturer training
- Equipment certification
- Technical workshops

**Category B: Business Development (Maximum 4 CECs per cycle)**
- Business management courses
- Marketing training
- Financial planning
- Leadership development

**Category C: Industry Participation (Maximum 4 CECs per cycle)**
- Conference attendance
- Trade show participation
- Association membership
- Industry committee service

**Category D: Self-Directed Learning (Maximum 2 CECs per cycle)**
- Industry publications
- Online research
- Webinars
- Technical reading

## Part 4: CEC Tracking and Recording

### Automatic Tracking

**DR-NRPG Platform Integration:**
- Platform training auto-logs to CARSI
- Completion certificates generated automatically
- CECs credited within 48 hours
- Email confirmation sent

**External Training Submission:**
1. Complete training with accredited provider
2. Obtain certificate of completion
3. Log into CARSI dashboard
4. Submit "External Training" form
5. Upload certificate
6. Await verification (3-5 business days)

### Manual CEC Entry

**For Category D (Self-Directed):**

1. **Technical Reading:**
   - Document: Article title, publication, date
   - Minimum 2,000 words
   - 0.5 CECs per article (max 2 CECs)

2. **Webinars:**
   - Minimum 1 hour duration
   - Provider confirmation required
   - 1 CEC per hour (max 2 CECs)

3. **Industry Research:**
   - Document research topic and findings
   - Minimum 4 hours effort
   - 1 CEC per project (max 2 CECs)

**Submission Requirements:**
- Summary of learning (minimum 200 words)
- Date completed
- Time invested
- Relevance to certification

## Part 5: Certification Renewal Process

### Renewal Timeline

**6 Months Before Expiry:**
- CARSI sends renewal reminder
- CEC balance check
- Gap analysis provided

**3 Months Before Expiry:**
- Second reminder sent
- Recommended training list
- Early renewal discount available

**1 Month Before Expiry:**
- Final reminder
- Urgent action required
- Late fees apply after expiry

### Renewal Steps

**Step 1: Verify CEC Balance**
```
Login to CARSI → Dashboard → CEC Summary
Required: 14 CECs
Current Balance: [Your Balance]
Status: [Compliant/Non-Compliant]
```

**Step 2: Complete Any Missing CECs**
- Review gap analysis
- Enroll in required training
- Complete before expiry

**Step 3: Submit Renewal Application**
- Online form at carsi.com.au/renewal
- Pay renewal fee: $165 (AUD)
- Confirm contact details
- Update insurance information

**Step 4: Verification**
- CARSI verifies CECs (5-7 business days)
- Background check (if required)
- Insurance verification

**Step 5: Renewal Confirmation**
- New certification issued
- Digital badge updated
- Physical certificate mailed
- DR-NRPG profile updated

### Renewal Fees

| Renewal Type | Fee | Late Fee | Total if Late |
|--------------|-----|----------|---------------|
| Standard Renewal | $165 | - | $165 |
| Late Renewal (0-6 months) | $165 | $85 | $250 |
| Reinstatement (6+ months) | $165 | $250 | $415 |
