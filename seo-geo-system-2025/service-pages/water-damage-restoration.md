## FAQ Schema Content
### Common Questions
1. **"How quickly should water damage be addressed?"**
   - Answer: Within 24-48 hours to prevent secondary damage like mold
   - Include Brisbane-specific humidity factors

2. **"Will my insurance cover water damage restoration?"**
   - Answer: Depends on cause - sudden burst pipes usually covered
   - Reference common Queensland insurance policies

3. **"How long does water damage restoration take?"**
   - Answer: 3-5 days for drying, 1-2 weeks for full restoration
   - Include factors affecting timeline

4. **"Can you save my hardwood floors after flooding?"**
   - Answer: Often yes with immediate professional attention
   - Detail cupping vs. crowning assessment
