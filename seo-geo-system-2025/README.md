## Key 2025/26 SEO Ranking Factors
### Local SEO (Google/Bing Maps)
- Google Business Profile Optimization (keyword-rich, media, engagement, accurate categories)
- Review quantity, quality, and frequency (respond, manage, request proactive feedback)
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all directories
- Proximity (optimize for your local footprint, including sub-regions & suburbs)
- Rich service descriptions & geo-content (local guides, emergency checklists, FAQs)
- High-authority local backlinks and geo-based citations
- Real media assets (videos/pics of before/after, staff/team, trucks on site)
- Schema markup implementation (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Review, Event)
- Mobile-first, fast loading, and Core Web Vitals

### GEO SEO (AI, Generative Engine Optimization)
- Structured tables, processes, Q&A, summaries for citation
- Consistent entity organization and metadata
- Author bios and credential info (demonstrate E-E-A-T)
- Incorporate direct statistics, reputable external data, and topical authority
- FAQ, HowTo, and list schema for AI/LLM rich results
- Context-rich citation strategy (in-article, schema.org, outbound links)
- Robust Google Maps integration with geoshapes, service area overlays

## Chris Palmer Proven Strategies & Methodologies
1. **Google Business Profile Optimization**
   - Use targeted main service + location keywords
   - Fill all GBP sections; update with events, site posts weekly
   - Encourage/solicit customer reviews (incentive, email follow-up)
   - Respond, ask and answer Q&A; use review keywords for SEO

2. **Hyperlocal Content and Service Area Pages**
   - Launch dedicated pages for every suburb, city, and major district
   - Integrate local landmarks, events, disaster stats
   - Regularly refresh, update local statistics/content (flood risk, insurance advisories)

3. **Review Generation and Management**
   - Implement automated SMS/email review requests post-service
   - Reference neighbourhoods and streets in reviews when possible
   - Reply to all reviews, address negative feedback with specific resolution plan

4. **Schema Markup and FAQ/HowTo**
   - Add LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Review, and Event schema sitewide
   - Structure location pages to answer top user queries, e.g., "How much does flood restoration cost in North Brisbane?"
   - Feature internal experts/staff (IICRC credential schema)

5. **GEO Optimization For AI/LLM**
   - Comparison tables and expert commentary sections (e.g. "How we compare to competitor X")
   - Structured emergency checklists, service maps, downloadable PDFs
   - Use entity-based content with explicit relationships (e.g., water damage→floodplain→Brisbane River risk map)

6. **Internal Linking & Technical SEO**
   - Link emergency pages, guides, FAQs from homepage and main service area pages
   - Ensure logical folder structure; service/region pairing in URLs (e.g. /brisbane/water-damage-restoration/)
   - Optimize robots.txt, XML sitemap, canonical tags

7. **Community Engagement and Backlinks**
   - Sponsor or feature local events, donate cleaning or restoration, partner with councils, emergency bodies
   - Secure backlinks from local media, council sites, business directories, real estate blogs
   - Engage industry associations: RIA, IAQAA, etc. for authority mentions
