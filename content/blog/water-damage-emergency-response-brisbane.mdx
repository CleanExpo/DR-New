## First 5 Minutes: Critical Actions

### 1. Ensure Safety First (0-2 Minutes)

**Electrical Hazards**
- Do NOT enter standing water if electrical outlets or appliances are submerged
- Turn off power at the switchboard if safe to access
- Avoid touching electrical switches with wet hands
- Keep children and pets away from affected areas
- If uncertain about electrical safety, wait for professionals

**Structural Hazards**
- Watch for sagging ceilings (may collapse from water weight)
- Avoid areas with water staining on ceilings
- Stay away from bulging walls
- Be cautious on wet stairs (slip hazard)
- Exit immediately if you hear creaking or groaning

**Contaminated Water**
- Sewage backups or flood water are health hazards
- Do not touch contaminated water with bare hands
- Keep children and pets away from black water
- Wear gloves and boots if you must enter
- Wash thoroughly if exposed to contaminated water

### 2. Stop the Water Source (2-3 Minutes)

**If Water Is From Internal Plumbing**
- Locate and turn off the water main (usually front of property near meter)
- Turn off water supply to specific fixture if identifiable
- Place buckets under active leaks
- Turn off hot water system if it's the source
- Open external taps to drain remaining water from pipes

**If Water Is From Appliances**
- Turn off washing machine, dishwasher, or faulty appliance
- Disconnect water supply hoses if accessible
- Unplug electrical appliances from safe distance
- Close valves behind appliances if present

**If Water Is From External Source**
- Clear blocked gutters causing overflow (if safe)
- Move garden hoses away from house
- Check for rising groundwater or flooding
- Document water source with photos

## Next 30 Minutes: Damage Mitigation

### 4. Remove Standing Water (If Safe)

**DIY Water Removal**
Use what you have available:
- Mops and buckets for small amounts
- Wet/dry vacuum if you own one
- Towels and old sheets to absorb water
- Squeegee water toward drains
- Wring out absorbed water frequently

**Leave to Professionals**
Do not attempt DIY removal if:
- Water is more than 2-3cm deep
- Area is larger than 10 square meters
- Water is contaminated (sewage or flooding)
- Electrical hazards are present
- Ceiling or walls are saturated

**Brisbane Note**
Don't underestimate Brisbane's humidity - even if you remove surface water, hidden moisture causes major problems. Professional extraction and drying is essential.

### 5. Move and Protect Contents

**Immediate Removal**
Move these items away from water immediately:
- Electronics (computers, TVs, stereo equipment)
- Important documents and photos
- Valuable artwork and collectibles
- Furniture with fabric upholstery
- Clothing and textiles
- Books and papers

**Elevate What Can't Be Moved**
- Place aluminum foil or plastic under furniture legs
- Use blocks or bricks to elevate heavy furniture
- Lift curtains and drapes away from floor
- Remove items from lower cabinets and drawers

**Don't Touch**
Leave these for professionals:
- Antiques or valuable furniture (can be damaged by moving)
- Built-in furniture and cabinetry
- Electronics that were submerged (electrical hazard)
- Items in severely flooded areas
- Anything covered in sewage or contaminated water

### 6. Increase Air Circulation

**Open Windows and Doors**
If weather permits:
- Open all windows to create cross-ventilation
- Open internal doors between rooms
- Use external doors to create airflow
- Exception: Don't open if humid or raining outside

**Use Fans**
If electricity is safe:
- Position household fans toward wet areas
- Point fans out windows to exhaust moisture
- Create air movement across wet surfaces
- Keep fans running continuously
- Do NOT use if any electrical hazards exist

**Air Conditioning**
- Set to dry or dehumidify mode if available
- Lower temperature to remove humidity
- Keep running continuously
- Professional dehumidifiers are far more effective

### Understanding Moisture Migration

**Why Professional Drying Matters**

Water doesn't just stay on surfaces - it migrates:
- Absorbs into walls, floors, and ceilings
- Travels through wall cavities
- Soaks into insulation and subfloors
- Spreads to adjacent rooms through capillary action
- Creates hidden pockets of moisture

**Brisbane's Humidity Challenge**

Our subtropical climate makes DIY drying nearly impossible:
- Outdoor humidity often 70-90%
- Air can't absorb moisture effectively
- Fans just circulate humid air
- Mould growth accelerates in warm, humid conditions
- Professional low-grain dehumidifiers essential

**The 72-Hour Window**

Critical timing for Brisbane properties:
- 0-24 hours: Extract water and begin drying
- 24-48 hours: Aggressive drying continues
- 48-72 hours: Mould spores begin germinating
- After 72 hours: Mould growth visible, secondary damage increases
- Professional intervention within 24 hours prevents most issues

## Prevention: Protecting Your Brisbane Home

### Regular Maintenance Prevents Emergencies

**Monthly Checks**
- Run water in all taps and showers (prevents trap drying)
- Check under sinks for leaks
- Inspect hot water system for corrosion or leaks
- Check washing machine hoses for bulging or cracks
- Look for water stains on ceilings and walls

**Annual Inspections**
Before Brisbane's wet season (October-March):
- Professional roof inspection
- Clean gutters and downpipes
- Check window and door seals
- Inspect grading around house (slopes away from foundation)
- Service hot water system

**Every 3-5 Years**
- Replace washing machine and dishwasher hoses
- Inspect bathroom waterproofing
- Check concrete slab for cracks (if slab-on-ground)
- Consider pipe inspection (especially if pre-1990s plumbing)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I use my household fans and dehumidifier?
Yes, any air movement helps while waiting for professional help. However, household equipment is insufficient for thorough drying. Professional equipment is 10-20 times more effective. Think of it as a band-aid before proper treatment.

### How long until mould starts growing?
Mould spores begin germinating within 24-48 hours in Brisbane's climate. Visible growth typically appears within 72-96 hours. This is why our guarantee includes response within 60 minutes - timing is critical.

### Can I sleep in my house during water damage restoration?
This depends on extent and location. Minor damage in one bathroom - usually yes. Extensive damage throughout house - probably not ideal. Contaminated water - definitely not until professionally remediated. We'll provide honest advice based on your situation.

### Will insurance cover water damage restoration?
Most sudden and accidental water damage is covered (burst pipes, storm damage, appliance failures). Gradual damage from poor maintenance usually isn't. Contact your insurer within 24-48 hours. We work with all major insurers and can help with claims process.

### How do you dry inside walls without removing plasterboard?
We use injecti-dry systems and wall cavity drying equipment to dry without removal if possible. Thermal imaging confirms drying progress. If walls are heavily saturated or water is contaminated, removal may be necessary for proper drying and inspection.

### What's the difference between water damage categories?
Category 1 is clean water (supply lines, rain). Category 2 is grey water with some contamination (appliances, toilets without solid waste). Category 3 is black water (sewage, flooding, or any water sitting 72+ hours). The category determines safety protocols and what can be salvaged.

### Do I need to be home during the drying process?
No, once equipment is set up and running. We'll need access for daily checks and moisture monitoring. Many clients go to work or temporary accommodation while equipment runs. We're fully insured and bonded.

### How much does emergency water damage restoration cost?
Small residential water damage: $2,000-5,000. Medium water damage: $5,000-15,000. Large or complex restoration: $15,000-50,000+. Cost depends on water volume, category, affected materials, and response time. Insurance typically covers most or all costs for covered events.
