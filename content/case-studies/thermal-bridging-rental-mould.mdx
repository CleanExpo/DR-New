## Understanding Thermal Bridging

### The Physics of Mould

**How Thermal Bridging Creates Mould:**

1. **Temperature Differential**
   - Roof heats to 60°C+ in sun
   - Interior surfaces stay cool
   - No insulation barrier

2. **Dew Point Reached**
   - Warm air meets cold surface
   - Water vapour condenses
   - Moisture accumulates

3. **Mould Growth Cycle**
   - Moisture provides habitat
   - Temperature fluctuates daily
   - Mould establishes permanently
