# Storm Water vs Clean Water: A $45,000 Difference

## Case Study: Brisbane Storm Drain Backup

When heavy storms overwhelmed Brisbane's city drains, contaminated water backed up through underground sub-drains into this family home. The difference between this and a simple pipe burst? About $45,000 in restoration costs.

## The Critical Distinction: Category 3 Water Damage

### What Happened Here

This Brisbane property suffered what we classify as **Category 3 water damage** - also known as "black water." The storm water entered through:

- City drain backup during heavy storms
- Underground sub-drain infiltration
- Migration under timber flooring
- Contamination spreading through kitchen

### Why This Matters for Your Claim

**Category 3 (Black Water) Requirements:**
- Complete disposal of ALL affected materials
- Timber flooring - cannot be restored
- Kitchen carcasses - full replacement required
- Wall linings - removal to 600mm minimum
- Contents - disposal of all exposed items
- Professional decontamination required

**If This Was Clean Water:**
- Timber floors often restorable with specialized drying
- Kitchen kickboards - minor repairs only
- Wall linings - drying without removal
- Contents - cleaning and restoration possible
- Standard drying equipment sufficient

## When to Get Expert Assessment

### Red Flags for Contaminated Water

- Any flooding from outside sources
- Toilet or sewage involvement
- Standing water over 48 hours
- Visible contamination or odor
- Water through ground level

### Clean Water Scenarios

- Hot water system failures
- Roof leaks (rain water)
- Appliance malfunctions
- Air conditioning drainage
