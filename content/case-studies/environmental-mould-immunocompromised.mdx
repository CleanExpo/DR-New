## Why Exterior Mould Enters Homes

### The Infiltration Pathways

**How Spores Travel Inside:**
1. **Door/Window Gaps** - Primary entry during opening
2. **Ventilation Systems** - HVAC intake points
3. **Clothing Transfer** - Spores attach to fabric
4. **Air Pressure** - Negative pressure draws spores in
5. **Pet Transfer** - Animals carry spores inside

### Brisbane's Perfect Mould Environment

**Climate Factors:**
- 60% average humidity year-round
- Warm temperatures accelerate growth
- Frequent rain events
- Limited sun exposure on patios
- Poor ventilation design
