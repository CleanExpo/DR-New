## Why Claims Are Being Denied

### ASIC Investigation Findings (2024)

<ExternalLink href="https://www.aph.gov.au/floodinsurance">Parliamentary Inquiry into Insurers' Responses</ExternalLink> revealed:

**Common Denial Tactics:**
1. **"Gradual vs Sudden"** - Claiming damage occurred slowly
2. **"Maintenance Issue"** - Blaming homeowner negligence
3. **"Flood vs Storm Water"** - Definition games
4. **"Pre-existing Damage"** - Using any prior issue as excuse
5. **"Policy Exclusions"** - Finding any loophole

**Shocking Statistics:**
- **1 in 6** complaints not even recorded by insurers
- **$850 million** repaid for pricing failures
- **$40 million** fine to Insurance Australia Ltd
- **75%** of AFCA decisions favor the insured (when they fight)

### Northern Australia Insurance Catastrophe

According to <ExternalLink href="https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/Northern%20Australia%20Insurance%20Inquiry%20-%20Final%20Report%20-%2030%20November%202020.pdf">ACCC Northern Australia Insurance Inquiry</ExternalLink>:

**Premium Explosions:**
- Median premiums: **$4,376** (WA), **$3,054** (NT), **$2,959** (QLD)

- **28%** increase in 12 months
- High-risk properties: **50%+** increases

**Coverage Crisis:**
- Only **23%** of high-risk homes have flood cover
- National average: **60%**
- **70%** of flood-risk households below median income
