## Understanding Mould in Brisbane Properties

### Why Mould Thrives in Brisbane

**Climate Factors**
- High humidity levels (especially summer)
- Subtropical temperatures ideal for growth
- Heavy rainfall and storm events
- Coastal moisture and sea breezes
- Limited air circulation in sealed buildings

**Common Mould Locations**

**Bathrooms & Ensuites**
Most common residential mould area:
- Shower recesses and tile grout
- Ceiling extractors and ventilation
- Behind bathroom cabinets
- Under silicone seals
- Around window frames

**Roof Spaces & Ceilings**
Often overlooked until severe:
- Inadequate roof ventilation
- Insulation moisture accumulation
- Roof leak aftermath
- Condensation from air conditioning
- Storm damage and water entry

**Subfloors & Crawl Spaces**
Particularly in Queensland homes:
- Poor subfloor ventilation
- Ground moisture and rising damp
- Plumbing leaks under house
- Flood aftermath
- Blocked or inadequate drainage

**Wall Cavities**
Hidden mould growth behind walls:
- Concealed plumbing leaks
- External water penetration
- Failed waterproofing membranes
- Condensation issues
- Previous water damage

**Other Problem Areas**
- Air conditioning systems and ducts
- Wardrobes and storage rooms
- Laundries and wet areas
- Around windows and external doors
- Behind furniture against external walls

### Health Risks of Mould Exposure

Mould can affect health, especially for sensitive individuals:

**Respiratory Issues**
- Asthma attacks and breathing difficulties
- Allergic reactions and hay fever
- Chronic coughing and wheezing
- Sinus congestion and infections
- Throat irritation

**Other Health Effects**
- Skin rashes and irritation
- Eye irritation and watering

- Immune system impacts
- Worsening of existing conditions

**High-Risk Groups**
- Young children and infants
- Elderly individuals

## Mould Prevention for Brisbane Properties

### Essential Ventilation

**Bathrooms**
- Install quality exhaust fans (rated for room size)
- Run fan during and 30 minutes after showers
- Open windows when possible
- Wipe down wet surfaces after use
- Fix any leaking taps or fixtures promptly

**Roof Spaces**
- Install whirlybird or ridge vents
- Ensure soffit vents are unblocked
- Check insulation is not blocking ventilation
- Inspect for roof leaks annually
- Consider roof space fans in problem areas

**Subfloors**
- Install adequate ventilation vents
- Keep vents clear of vegetation
- Consider subfloor fans if needed
- Ensure proper drainage away from house
- Check for and repair plumbing leaks

### Humidity Control

**Dehumidification**
- Use dehumidifiers in problem areas
- Target humidity below 60% (ideally 40-50%)
- Empty dehumidifier tanks regularly
- Consider whole-house solutions for severe cases

**Air Conditioning**
- Regular servicing and filter changes
- Ensure condensate drainage is working
- Check for duct leaks and condensation
- Don't set temperature too low (causes condensation)

### Regular Maintenance

**Monthly Checks**
- Inspect for visible mould growth
- Check bathroom and kitchen silicone seals
- Look for water stains on ceilings/walls
- Ensure exhaust fans are working
- Clean bathroom surfaces thoroughly

**Annual Inspections**
- Professional roof inspection
- Check gutters and downpipes
- Inspect external walls for damage
- Test window and door seals
- Check plumbing for leaks

### Quick Response to Water Damage

**Immediate Actions**
- Extract standing water immediately
- Begin drying within 24-48 hours
- Use fans and dehumidifiers
- Remove wet materials if necessary
- Call professionals for significant water damage

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if I have a mould problem?
Visible mould growth, musty odours, water stains, or increased allergy symptoms are common indicators. However, mould can be hidden in wall cavities, roof spaces, or subfloors. If you've had water damage, high humidity, or suspect mould, professional assessment is recommended.

### Can I remove mould myself?
Small surface mould (less than 1 square meter) on non-porous surfaces may be cleaned with proper safety equipment. However, widespread mould, hidden mould, or mould from water damage requires professional remediation to ensure complete removal, address moisture sources, and prevent regrowth.

### How much does mould remediation cost in Brisbane?
Cost depends on extent and location of mould. Small bathroom mould treatment might cost $500-$1,500. Extensive remediation requiring containment and material removal can range from $3,000-$15,000+. We provide detailed quotes after assessment and work with insurance companies when applicable.

### How long does mould remediation take?
Simple surface mould cleaning takes 1-2 days. Moderate remediation with containment typically takes 3-5 days. Extensive remediation requiring structural work may take 1-3 weeks. We'll provide an accurate timeline after assessment.

### Will mould come back after remediation?
Professional remediation eliminates existing mould. Recurrence depends on addressing moisture sources and maintaining proper ventilation. We identify and recommend repairs for all moisture sources. Following our prevention advice significantly reduces the risk of mould returning.

### Is mould testing necessary?
Visual inspection is often sufficient for obvious mould. Testing may be recommended for hidden mould, identifying specific species, post-remediation verification, or insurance requirements. We'll advise if testing would benefit your situation.

### What's the difference between mould removal and remediation?
Removal refers to cleaning visible mould. Remediation is comprehensive: identifying moisture sources, containing affected areas, removing contaminated materials, treating all surfaces, addressing moisture issues, and preventing recurrence. Professional remediation is more effective and thorough.

### Can mould affect my property value?
Undisclosed mould can significantly impact property value and create legal issues when selling. Professional remediation with certification resolves the problem and provides documentation for future property transactions, protecting your investment.
