## Why Speed Matters in Flood Restoration

### The Critical First 24 Hours

**0-1 Hour**: Water spreads rapidly, doubling damage every hour
**1-4 Hours**: Furniture absorbs water, drywall swells, metal tarnishes
**4-8 Hours**: Furniture warps, paint blisters, mould begins
**8-24 Hours**: Mould spreads, wood swells, electronics fail
**24-48 Hours**: Structural damage, extensive mould, contamination
**48+ Hours**: Severe structural damage, hazardous conditions, major costs
