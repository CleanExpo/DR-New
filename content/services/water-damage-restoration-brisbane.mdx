## Prevent Water Damage - Expert Tips

### Regular Maintenance Checklist
Protect your Brisbane property with:
- **Roof & Gutters** - Clean before storm season (October-March)
- **Plumbing Inspection** - Annual check of pipes and fixtures
- **Hot Water System** - Service every 5 years
- **Appliance Hoses** - Replace washing machine hoses every 3-5 years
- **Drainage** - Ensure proper grading away from foundation

### Storm Season Preparation
Before Brisbane's wet season:
- Clear gutters and downpipes
- Inspect roof for damage or loose tiles
- Check window and door seals
- Trim overhanging tree branches
- Test sump pumps if applicable

### Early Warning Signs
Contact us immediately if you notice:
- Unexplained increase in water bills
- Musty odours or visible mould
- Discoloured patches on walls or ceilings
- Soft or spongy flooring
- Peeling paint or wallpaper

### What's the difference between water damage categories?
Category 1 is clean water from supply lines or rain. Category 2 (grey water) has some contamination from appliances or toilets. Category 3 (black water) is highly contaminated from sewage or flooding. The category determines the restoration approach, safety precautions, and what can be salvaged. Our team assesses the category and handles all types safely.
