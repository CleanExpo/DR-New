**Electrical Fires**
Particularly in older Brisbane homes:
- Outdated wiring in heritage properties

## Fire Prevention for Brisbane Properties

### Essential Fire Safety Measures

**Smoke Alarms**
Queensland legislation requires:
- Photoelectric smoke alarms in all homes
- Alarms in every bedroom
- Alarms in hallways connecting bedrooms
- Alarms on every storey
- Interconnected alarms (new builds from 2017)
- 10-year lithium battery or hardwired

**Fire Extinguishers**
Recommended for homes:
- Kitchen-rated extinguisher (Class F)
- General purpose extinguisher (ABE)
- Regular maintenance and testing
- Family training on proper use

**Regular Maintenance**
- Annual electrical safety inspections
- Dryer vent cleaning every 6 months
- Range hood filter cleaning monthly
- Chimney inspection and cleaning annually
- Check and test smoke alarms monthly

### Emergency Preparedness

**Family Fire Plan**
- Identify multiple escape routes
- Designate meeting point outside
- Practice evacuation twice yearly
- Ensure everyone knows emergency numbers
- Keep important documents in fireproof safe

**Property Protection**
- Clear vegetation from around house
- Store flammable materials safely
- Install fireproof document storage
- Keep garden hoses accessible
- Consider ember-proof vents and screens

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How quickly can you respond to fire damage emergencies?
Our emergency response team aims to arrive within 60 minutes for Brisbane CBD and inner suburbs like Hamilton, Ascot, and New Farm. We provide 24/7 service, 365 days a year, because fire damage requires immediate attention to prevent further deterioration.

### Can my property be restored after a fire?
Most properties can be successfully restored after fire damage, even severe fires. Our Master Restorer expertise includes rebuilding from catastrophic damage. After assessment, we'll provide an honest evaluation of what can be restored versus what requires replacement.

### How long does fire damage restoration take?
Minor smoke damage may be resolved in 1-2 weeks. Significant fire damage with structural rebuilding typically takes 2-4 months. Complex heritage property restoration may require longer. We'll provide an accurate timeline after our initial assessment and work efficiently to minimize disruption.

### Will my insurance cover fire damage restoration?
Most comprehensive home insurance policies cover fire damage, including structural repairs, contents replacement, and temporary accommodation. We work directly with all major insurers and can assist with your claim from initial notification through final settlement.

### Can smoke odour be completely removed?
Yes, with professional restoration techniques. We use thermal fogging, ozone treatment, and hydroxyl generators to eliminate smoke odour at the molecular level. Sometimes porous materials like insulation require replacement, but we can typically eliminate odours completely.

### What should I do immediately after a fire?
First, ensure everyone is safe and emergency services have been contacted. Then call us for emergency property securing. Don't enter until cleared by authorities. Notify your insurance company within 48 hours. Don't attempt to clean smoke damage yourself, as improper methods can make it worse.

### Do you provide emergency board-up services?
Yes, emergency property securing is part of our immediate response. We'll board up windows and doors, tarp damaged roofs, and arrange security if needed. This prevents further damage from weather, animals, and unauthorized entry while preventing insurance complications.

### Can you restore smoke-damaged contents?
Many contents can be professionally restored. We use ultrasonic cleaning, ozone chambers, and specialist techniques for different materials. We'll inventory all contents, assess each item, and either restore on-site or pack-out for specialist cleaning and secure storage.
