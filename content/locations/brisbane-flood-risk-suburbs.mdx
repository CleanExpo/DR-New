## The Insurance Reality Check

### What Insurance Companies Know (But Don't Tell You)

**Based on <ExternalLink href="https://www.qld.gov.au/emergency/dealing-disasters/disaster-types/flood/flood-prepare/flood-insurance">Queensland Government Flood Insurance Guide</ExternalLink>:**

1. **Definition Games**
   - "Flood" vs "Storm water" vs "Flash flooding"
   - Each defined differently in policies
   - Wrong definition = denied claim

2. **The Gradual Damage Trap**
   - Any delay in discovery = potential denial
   - Mould after 48 hours = "gradual"
   - Must prove "sudden and accidental"

3. **Premium Death Spiral**
   - High-risk suburbs seeing 200%+ increases
   - Some properties now uninsurable
   - Self-insurance becoming only option
