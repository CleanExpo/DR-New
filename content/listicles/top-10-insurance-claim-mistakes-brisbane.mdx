## 3. Using Wrong Terminology
**Cost: Complete claim denial**

### Fatal Words That Deny Claims

**NEVER Say:**
- "Flood" (if it's storm water)
- "Gradual" (always say sudden)
- "Mould" (say moisture damage)
- "Seepage" (say water intrusion)
- "Old damage" (say newly discovered)

**ALWAYS Say:**
- "Storm water" not "flood water"
- "Sudden discovery" not "gradual damage"
- "Water damage" not "mould damage"
- "Escape of liquid" for plumbing
- "Impact damage" for storms

### Real Denial Example
Brisbane family said "flood" instead of "storm water ingress" - claim denied. Same damage, wrong word = $0 payout.

## 5. DIY Repairs Before Assessment
**Cost: Entire claim voided**

### The Trap
Insurance requires opportunity to assess. ANY repairs = evidence destroyed = claim denied.

### What Voids Coverage
- Removing damaged materials
- Painting over water stains
- Professional cleaning
- Structural repairs
- Throwing anything away

### Milton Case Study
Homeowner cleaned mould, repainted. Insurance denied $85,000 claim - "no evidence of damage."

### Safe Actions Only
✅ Emergency tarps
✅ Turn off water/power
✅ Photo everything
✅ Move contents (document first)
✅ Prevent injury

## 10. Not Understanding Policy Exclusions
**Cost: 35% of claims denied**

### Top 5 Deadly Exclusions

**1. Gradual Damage Clause**
- Anything over 48 hours
- Kills most mould claims
- Solution: Prove sudden discovery

**2. Maintenance Exclusion**
- "Wear and tear"
- "Lack of maintenance"
- Solution: Document all maintenance

**3. Flood vs Storm Water**
- Different definitions
- Different coverage
- Solution: Use correct terms

**4. Unoccupied Property**
- 60+ days empty
- Coverage suspended
- Solution: Regular documented visits

**5. Building Code Upgrades**
- Old construction standards
- Compliance costs excluded
- Solution: Separate assessment needed

### How to Override Exclusions
- Expert reports
- Legal precedents
- AFCA complaints
- Policy ambiguity arguments
- Consumer protection laws
