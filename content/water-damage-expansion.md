## NEW SECTION 1: Common Causes of Water Damage in Brisbane (180 words)

### H2: Common Causes of Water Damage in Brisbane Homes and Businesses

Brisbane's subtropical climate and ageing infrastructure create unique water damage risks for property owners. Understanding these common causes helps you identify potential problems before they escalate into costly disasters.

**Storm and Heavy Rainfall**: Brisbane receives intense rainfall during summer months, overwhelming gutters and stormwater systems. Properties in flood-prone suburbs like Hamilton, Ascot, and New Farm require regular drainage maintenance to prevent water intrusion.

**Burst Pipes and Plumbing Failures**: Queensland's temperature fluctuations cause pipe expansion and contraction, leading to joint failures and ruptures. Older homes in established Brisbane suburbs often have galvanized pipes that corrode over time, increasing burst pipe risk.

**Roof Leaks**: Severe storms and ageing roof materials allow water penetration into ceiling cavities. Without immediate attention, roof leaks cause structural timber damage, insulation saturation, and extensive mould growth.

**Appliance Malfunctions**: Hot water systems, washing machines, and dishwashers commonly fail without warning. A single burst hot water system can release hundreds of litres, causing catastrophic damage within minutes.

**Air Conditioning Condensate Issues**: Brisbane's humid conditions mean air conditioners work overtime. Blocked condensate drains cause overflow into ceiling spaces and walls, creating hidden water damage and mould growth.
