# SEO Reports Directory

This directory contains comprehensive SEO research and analysis reports for the Disaster Recovery Brisbane website.

## Available Reports

### 1. Competitor Analysis - Brisbane
**File**: `competitor-analysis-brisbane.json`
**Date**: 2025-11-09
**Status**: Complete

#### Report Contents:
- **Top 5 Competitors Analyzed**:
  1. Steamatic Brisbane (National brand, IICRC certified)
  2. Reztor Restoration (Regional player, fast response)
  3. Premier Restoration Services (25+ years, local operator)
  4. South QLD Restoration (11+ locations, family-owned)
  5. Flood Services Brisbane (Flood specialist, 1-hour response)

- **8 Primary Keywords Analyzed**:
  - water damage restoration Brisbane
  - mold remediation Brisbane
  - emergency restoration Brisbane
  - flood damage restoration Brisbane
  - emergency water damage Brisbane
  - fire damage restoration Brisbane
  - IICRC Master Restorer Brisbane
  - storm damage repair Brisbane

- **Key Insights**:
  - Master Restorer positioning = MAJOR competitive advantage (only 3.7% of industry)
  - No competitors have Master level credentials in Brisbane
  - Steamatic is primary threat (national brand + strong testimonials)
  - High-net-worth suburb focus is untapped niche

- **Content Gaps Identified**:
  - Missing 12+ location-specific pages for affluent suburbs
  - Need 7+ service sub-pages (carpet drying, structural drying, etc.)
  - No blog for ongoing SEO content
  - Limited FAQ content (need 15+ questions)
  - Missing case studies and testimonials

- **Quick Wins Recommended**:
  1. Google My Business optimization
  2. FAQ schema implementation
  3. Location pages for Hamilton, Ascot, New Farm, Toowong
  4. Testimonial collection and display
  5. Mobile click-to-call optimization

- **4-Phase Action Plan Included**:
  - Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Critical GMB and mobile optimization
  - Phase 2 (Weeks 3-6): Location pages and service expansion
  - Phase 3 (Weeks 7-12): Blog launch and link building
  - Phase 4 (Month 4+): Ongoing content and monitoring

## Usage

These reports are designed to:
1. Inform SEO strategy and content creation
2. Identify competitive advantages and gaps
3. Guide technical SEO implementation
4. Provide actionable recommendations
5. Track competitor movements over time
