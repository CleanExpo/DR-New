The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC Australia) is the primary professional certification body for restoration contractors in Australia.

🚀 NRPG Partnership Levels

The DR-NRPG platform operates a tiered partnership system based on performance, tenure, and professional development. Higher tiers unlock better job allocation, reduced fees, and premium support.

CANDIDATE Level
Entry Level

Requirements: New contractors (0-6 months) | Merit score 40-54 | Minimum WRT certification

Benefits Included:

  • Standard job rotation access
  • Platform fee: 12% + GST
  • 24/7 support access
  • Basic training modules (20 NRPG courses)
  • Monthly payment processing
  • Profile listing in contractor directory
PARTNER Level
Standard

Requirements: 6+ months tenure | Merit score 55-69 | WRT + 1 additional cert | 50+ completed jobs

Additional Benefits:

  • Platform fee reduced to 11% + GST (save $1,000+ annually)

Requirements: 12+ months tenure | Merit score 70-84 | WRT + ASD + 1 more | 150+ jobs | 4.5+ star rating
