## Content Mission Statement
Create authoritative, trust-building content that positions our disaster recovery service as the exclusive choice for luxury property owners, commercial property managers, and insurance professionals in Brisbane's premium markets.

## Target Audience Personas

### Persona 1: Luxury Property Owner
- **Demographics**: 45-65, household income $500K+
- **Properties**: Hamilton waterfront, Ascot heritage home
- **Pain Points**: Protecting investments, maintaining property value
- **Content Needs**: Prevention guides, insurance maximization, case studies

### Persona 2: Commercial Property Manager
- **Demographics**: 35-50, manages $50M+ portfolio
- **Properties**: CBD offices, retail complexes
- **Pain Points**: Minimizing downtime, tenant satisfaction
- **Content Needs**: Emergency protocols, compliance guides, ROI data

### Persona 3: Strata Manager
- **Demographics**: 30-45, manages 20+ properties
- **Properties**: Luxury apartments, mixed-use developments
- **Pain Points**: Coordinating multiple stakeholders, budget management
- **Content Needs**: Process guides, communication templates, cost management

### Persona 4: Insurance Professional
- **Demographics**: 30-55, handles high-value claims
- **Properties**: Various premium residential and commercial
- **Pain Points**: Claim accuracy, restoration quality, timelines
- **Content Needs**: Technical specifications, compliance documentation, case studies
