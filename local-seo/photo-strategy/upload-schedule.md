## 📋 PHOTO OPTIMIZATION CHECKLIST

### Before Upload
- [ ] Resize to optimal dimensions (1200x900 minimum)
- [ ] Compress file size (<5MB, ideally <2MB)
- [ ] Add location EXIF data where applicable
- [ ] Rename file with SEO keywords
- [ ] Prepare caption with keywords
- [ ] Include relevant hashtags

### Caption Formula
```
[Action/Description] + [Location if relevant] + [Benefit/Result] + [Call to action if appropriate]

Example: "IICRC certified technicians restoring water damage in Ascot - Brisbane families trust our 24/7 emergency response. Call 1300 309 361"
```

### Keyword Integration
Every caption should include 2-3 of these:
- Location: Brisbane, Ipswich, [Specific Suburb]
- Service: water damage, fire restoration, mould removal
- Trust: IICRC, certified, insurance approved
- Urgency: 24/7, emergency, rapid response
- Local: family-owned, local, Brisbane-based

## 📊 PHOTO PERFORMANCE TRACKING

### Key Metrics
- Views per photo
- Engagement (likes/shares)
- Click-through to profile
- Correlation with calls/directions

### A/B Testing Elements
- Professional vs candid shots
- Before/after vs action shots
- Team photos vs equipment
- Tight crops vs wide shots

### Photo Rotation Strategy
- Monitor views after 30 days
- Replace photos with <50 views
- Keep high-performers as featured
- Test different angles of same subject

## 📝 PHOTO COMPLIANCE

### Must Follow
- Customer privacy (no faces without permission)
- Property identification (blur house numbers)
- Insurance confidentiality
- Safety standards visible
- Professional appearance

### Avoid
- Graphic damage images
- Identifiable valuables
- Customer distress
- Unsafe practices
- Poor lighting/quality
