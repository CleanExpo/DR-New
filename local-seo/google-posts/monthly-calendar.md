## 🎯 POST OPTIMIZATION GUIDELINES

### Optimal Posting Times
- **Monday**: 8:00 AM (catch morning browsers)
- **Wednesday**: 12:00 PM (lunch break visibility)
- **Friday**: 3:00 PM (weekend planning time)

### Image Requirements
- **Dimensions**: 1200x900px minimum
- **Format**: JPG (smaller file size)
- **Content**: Before/after, team photos, equipment
- **Branding**: Logo watermark on all images

### CTA Distribution
- **Learn More**: 40% (education/trust)
- **Call Now**: 30% (direct response)
- **Book Now**: 20% (appointments)
- **View Gallery**: 10% (social proof)

### Keyword Integration
Each post should naturally include:
- Location (Brisbane, Ipswich, suburb names)
- Service (water damage, fire, mould, etc.)
- Urgency terms (emergency, 24/7, rapid)
- Trust signals (IICRC, insurance, certified)
