## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE NOTES

### Must Include
- Unsubscribe option
- Company details
- Physical address
- Direct contact method

### Must Avoid
- Incentivizing reviews
- Asking only happy customers
- Review gating
- Fake urgency
- Misleading subject lines
