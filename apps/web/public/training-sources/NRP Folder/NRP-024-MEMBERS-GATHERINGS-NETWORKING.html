Forum Best Practices

  • ✅ Search before posting - Check if your question has been answered previously
  • ✅ Use descriptive titles - "Help with Desiccant vs LGR?" better than "Equipment Question"
  • ✅ Include context - Job type, location, equipment used, issue encountered
  • ✅ Add photos when relevant - Visual problems get faster, more accurate responses
  • ✅ Mark solutions - Mark helpful replies as "Solution" to help future members
  • ✅ Give back to community - Answer questions in your area of expertise
  • ✅ Stay professional - Respectful discourse, no competitor bashing, constructive feedback only

Maximising Your Networking ROI

  1. Set clear networking goals - Define what you want to achieve (referral partners, equipment advice, marketing insights)
  2. Prepare your elevator pitch - 30-second intro covering who you are, what you do, what makes you different
  3. Focus on giving first - Offer help, introductions, or advice before asking for anything
  4. Follow up within 48 hours - Send connection requests, LinkedIn invites, or emails while you're fresh in their mind
  5. Track your connections - Use CRM or spreadsheet to note where you met, conversation topics, follow-up items
  6. Attend consistently - Relationships build over time; attend multiple events to deepen connections
  7. Join online between events - Stay active in forum to maintain relationships when not meeting face-to-face
  8. Bring business cards - Always have cards ready; include QR code linking to your DR-NRPG profile
  9. Dress professionally - First impressions matter; business casual minimum for all NRPG events
  10. Turn connections into collaborations - Move beyond small talk to explore genuine partnership opportunities
