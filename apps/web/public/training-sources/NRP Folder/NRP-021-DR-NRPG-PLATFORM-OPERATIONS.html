🔄 DR-NRPG Rotation Algorithm Explained

The DR-NRPG platform uses a sophisticated merit-based rotation algorithm to ensure fair job distribution across all qualified contractors in each service area.

How the Rotation Algorithm Works

1

Job Request Received

Client submits job request through DR-NRPG platform (web, mobile, or insurance partner API). Job details include location, type, urgency level, and estimated scope.

2

Geographic Filtering

System identifies all contractors within service radius of job location. Default radius: 50km urban, 100km regional, 200km remote areas (Australian geographic classification).

3

Competency Matching

Filters contractors by required certifications (IICRC), specialisation (water/fire/mould), and equipment capabilities. Only qualified contractors proceed to rotation.

4

Merit Score Calculation

Each contractor assigned a dynamic merit score based on:
• Response time history (30%)
• Job completion rating (25%)
• Client satisfaction score (20%)
• Time since last job (15%)
• Platform tenure and training (10%)

5

Priority Ranking

Contractors ranked by merit score. Top 3 contractors offered job simultaneously. First to accept wins the job. If all decline, next 3 contractors offered job.

6

Job Assignment & Notification

Winning contractor receives job assignment via SMS, email, and push notification. Must confirm acceptance within 15 minutes for emergency jobs, 60 minutes for standard jobs.

⚡ Emergency Job Priority

Emergency jobs (flood, fire, urgent water damage) bypass standard rotation and notify ALL qualified contractors within radius simultaneously. First responder gets the job. Emergency response affects merit score significantly (+20 points for acceptance within 5 minutes).

✅ Job Acceptance Workflow

Step-by-Step Acceptance Process

Step 1: Job Notification Received
You receive job notification via SMS, email, and push notification (if app installed). Notification includes job ID, location, type, and response deadline.
Step 2: Review Job Details
Log into dashboard to view complete job details including client information, scope of works, photos, special requirements, and estimated value.
Step 3: Assess Capacity & Availability
Check your schedule, equipment availability, team capacity, and travel time. Consider current workload and ability to meet response time requirements.
Step 4: Accept or Decline
Click "Accept Job" or "Decline Job" button. If declining, select reason (too far, no capacity, outside expertise, other). Reason affects merit score minimally if valid.
Step 5: Confirmation & Client Notification
System immediately notifies client of acceptance. Your contact details shared with client. Job appears in "Active Jobs" section. Initial contact required within 60 minutes.
Step 6: Initial Client Contact
Call or SMS client within 60 minutes to confirm attendance time, discuss requirements, and answer questions. Log contact in platform for audit trail.

⏰ Response Time Requirements

Emergency Jobs: 15 minutes to accept, 2 hours to attend site
Urgent Jobs: 60 minutes to accept, 4 hours to attend site
Standard Jobs: 60 minutes to accept, 24 hours to attend site
Scheduled Jobs: 24 hours to accept, attend at scheduled time

✅ Best Practices for Platform Success

Top 10 Platform Best Practices

  1. Enable all notification methods - SMS, email, and push notifications ensure you never miss job offers
  2. Keep your availability calendar updated - Block out holidays, maintenance days, and periods of unavailability
  3. Respond to all jobs within 30 minutes - Even declines help the algorithm work efficiently
  4. Maintain 90%+ acceptance rate - Only decline if genuinely unable to service the job
  5. Submit invoices within 24 hours - Faster invoicing = faster payment cycles
  6. Upload high-quality photos - Clear, well-lit photos improve client satisfaction and reduce disputes
  7. Communicate proactively - Update job status regularly, notify clients of delays immediately
  8. Complete NRPG training modules - Improves merit score and keeps your skills current
  9. Request client reviews - Positive reviews significantly boost merit score and build credibility
  10. Use RestoreAssist.ai for all estimates - Standardised estimates reduce disputes and speed up payment processing
