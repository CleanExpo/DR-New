Australian Emergency Response Framework

Priority Level Scenario Response Time Resources Notification
Priority 1 - Critical Active flooding, structural damage 30 minutes Full crew + equipment Immediate to all parties
Priority 2 - Urgent Significant water damage, smoke 2 hours Standard crew Within 30 minutes
Priority 3 - Standard Contained damage, stable 4 hours Assessment team Within 1 hour
Priority 4 - Scheduled Minor damage, preventive 24 hours Single technician Business hours
CAT Event Natural disaster, multiple sites Immediate All available Emergency broadcast

Emergency Response Timeline

0-5 Minutes
Receive call, gather initial information, dispatch confirmation
5-15 Minutes
Mobilise crew, load equipment, depart for site
15-30 Minutes
En route updates, prepare documentation, coordinate resources
30-45 Minutes
Arrive on site, safety assessment, establish work zones
45-60 Minutes
Begin mitigation, document damage, communicate with stakeholders
60+ Minutes
Continue work, provide updates, adjust resources as needed

⚠ Critical Safety Protocol

STOP - LOOK - ASSESS - CONTROL

  • STOP: Do not enter until safe
  • LOOK: Identify all hazards
  • ASSESS: Evaluate risks and resources
  • CONTROL: Implement safety measures before work
