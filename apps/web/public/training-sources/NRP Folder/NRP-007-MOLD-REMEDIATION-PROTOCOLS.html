NRP-007: Mould Remediation Protocols

IICRC S520 Standards & Australian Guidelines

1. Mould Remediation Conditions (IICRC S520)

Condition 1 - Normal Fungal Ecology

Definition: Indoor environment with settled spores, fungal fragments, or traces consistent with normal fungal ecology

Action Required: No remediation needed - maintain normal cleaning

Moisture Levels: <16% wood moisture content, <80% surface RH

Visual Indicators: No visible mould growth, no water damage

Condition 2 - Settled Contamination

Definition: Indoor environment contaminated with settled spores not from growth in that area

Action Required: HEPA vacuuming, damp wiping, source removal

Moisture Levels: May be normal but contamination from elsewhere

Visual Indicators: Dust/debris with elevated spore counts, no active growth

Condition 3 - Active Growth

Definition: Indoor environment with actual mould growth and associated spores

Action Required: Full containment, removal, and remediation

Moisture Levels: >16% wood moisture, >80% surface RH

Visual Indicators: Visible mould growth, musty odor, water damage

Contamination Size Area Coverage Containment Required PPE Level Clearance Testing
Level I <10 sq ft (1 m²) Minimal - plastic sheeting N95, gloves, goggles Visual inspection
Level II 10-100 sq ft (1-10 m²) Critical barriers, HEPA N95/P100, suit, gloves Visual + optional testing
Level III 100-1000 sq ft (10-100 m²) Full containment, NAM Full-face respirator, suit Mandatory clearance test
Level IV >1000 sq ft (>100 m²) Multiple containments Full PPE, decon chamber Third-party clearance
Australian Requirement: Under Safe Work Australia guidelines, mould remediation over 10m² requires licensed asbestos removalists if building materials contain asbestos (pre-1990 buildings). Always test first!

4. Australian Mould Species & Health Impacts

Species Common Locations Health Impact Remediation Priority
Stachybotrys chartarum Water-damaged drywall, ceiling tiles Toxic - respiratory issues, bleeding Immediate - Full PPE
Aspergillus fumigatus HVAC systems, insulation Allergenic/Infectious - aspergillosis High - Medical monitoring
Penicillium chrysogenum Carpet, wallpaper, insulation Allergenic - asthma, hay fever Moderate - Standard PPE
Cladosporium herbarum Window frames, bathrooms Allergenic - skin/eye irritation Low-Moderate
Alternaria alternata Showers, under sinks Allergenic - asthma trigger Moderate
Australian Climate Note: Queensland and Northern Territory's tropical climate creates year-round mould risk. Maintain indoor humidity below 60% and temperature below 25°C to prevent growth.
