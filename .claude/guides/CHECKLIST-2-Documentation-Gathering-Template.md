For each item, locate and attach one or more of:

#### Bank/Credit Card Statements
- [ ] Statement showing purchase transaction

- [ ] Full receipt with itemization
- [ ] Date and vendor information
- [ ] Price clearly shown

## Final Checklist

Before submitting claim documentation, confirm:

- [ ] All documents are originals or certified copies
- [ ] Documents are legible (not faded, damaged, or blurry)
- [ ] All handwritten sections are clear and readable
- [ ] Photos are high resolution (not compressed)
- [ ] Sensitive information is protected (passport #, account #, etc.)
- [ ] Documents are organized logically
- [ ] Index or list of included documents provided
- [ ] Duplicate/backup copy retained for your records
- [ ] Digital copies stored securely
- [ ] Submission method confirmed with insurer
- [ ] Confirmation of receipt obtained from insurer

---

**Format**: Can be printed and filled by hand, or completed digitally

**Retention**: Keep copies of all documentation for minimum 7 years

**Digital Storage**: Recommended to use cloud backup (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox)

**Spreadsheet**: Create separate Excel spreadsheet for detailed item tracking with above table structure
