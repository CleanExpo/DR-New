## PART 1: LEGAL COMPLIANCE REVIEW

### 1.1 Insurance Content Compliance Checklist

**Objective**: Verify all insurance information is accurate, current, and compliant with Australian law

**Responsible Party**: Legal Advisor / Insurance Compliance Specialist
**Timeline**: Week 1 (Days 1-5)
**Success Criteria**: Sign-off from legal advisor required before proceeding

#### Legal Review Items

**Federal Legislation Compliance**:
- [ ] Insurance Contracts Act 1984 (Cth) - "Utmost good faith" principle accurately represented
- [ ] General Insurance Code of Practice 2020 (Oct 2023 update) - All timeframes current:
  - [ ] 3-day acknowledgment requirement verified
  - [ ] 10-day response requirement verified
  - [ ] 20-day progress update requirement verified
  - [ ] 4-month standard decision timeframe verified
  - [ ] 12-month exceptional decision timeframe verified
  - [ ] 30-day complaint resolution requirement verified
- [ ] AFCA (Australian Financial Complaints Authority) - All information current:
  - [ ] Jurisdiction limits ($631,500 cap) verified
  - [ ] Escalation trigger (30 days without resolution) correct
  - [ ] Contact information current (1800 931 678)
  - [ ] Decision binding on insurers, optional for consumers correctly stated

**Insurer-Specific Requirements**:
- [ ] NRMA: 2+ photo requirement accurate
- [ ] Suncorp: 3+ photos per damage area accurate
- [ ] Allianz: Flood cover as standard (not optional) verified
- [ ] AAMI: Flood cover as standard verified
- [ ] QBE: Coverage requirements accurate
- [ ] IAG: Coverage requirements accurate
- [ ] CGU: Coverage requirements accurate
- [ ] Medibank: Coverage requirements accurate

**Coverage & Exclusions**:
- [ ] Mandatory coverage items (fire, storm, malicious damage, etc.) are complete
- [ ] Optional coverage items (flood, accidental damage, etc.) clearly marked as "optional"
- [ ] Universal exclusions (wear & tear, poor maintenance, deliberate acts) are universal
- [ ] Insurer-specific exclusions don't contradict sourced information

**Consumer Rights**:
- [ ] Cooling-off period (14-21 days) is accurate
- [ ] AFCA escalation rights correctly stated
- [ ] Complaint process correctly described
- [ ] Cancellation rights accurately stated
- [ ] No misleading information about coverage or rights

**Professional Indemnity Insurance**:
- [ ] NSW mandate (July 1, 2026) accurately represented
- [ ] Applies to "Design and Building Practitioners" correctly scoped
- [ ] Not overstated as universal requirement

**Disclaimers & Limitations**:
- [ ] Platform clearly states it's not insurance advice
- [ ] Contractors cannot advise on coverage (properly stated)
- [ ] Clients directed to insurers for final coverage decisions
- [ ] Educational material labeled as such

#### Sign-Off Process

**Step 1: Legal Review**
```

- [Any issues identified]
- [Recommendations]
```

**Step 2: Corrections (if needed)**
- Document any required changes

## PART 2: USER TESTING WITH CONTRACTORS

### 2.1 Contractor User Testing Plan

**Objective**: Validate training modules, platform features, and insurance integration with actual contractors

**Participant Group**: 10 contractors (mix of experience levels)
**Test Duration**: 3-5 days (Week 1-2)
**Focus Areas**: Usability, content clarity, relevance, feature functionality

#### Recruitment Criteria

**Participant Selection**:
- Mix of contractor types (water, fire, mould, general)
- Experience levels: 2 new contractors, 5 experienced, 3 expert level
- Geographic diversity: At least 3 different states
- IICRC certification levels: Mix of Technician, Supervisor, Inspector
- Tech comfort: Mix of high-tech and traditional users

**Recruitment Method**:
1. Email invitation to 20 NRPG contractors
2. Selection based on availability + criteria match
3. Incentive: $100 gift card for completion
4. Scheduled in small groups (2-3 contractors)

#### Test Scenarios
