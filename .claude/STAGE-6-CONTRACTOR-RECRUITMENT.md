## RECRUITMENT STRATEGY

### Target Contractor Profile

**Primary Criteria** (Must have):
- Active NRPG contractor (last job <3 months)
- Available for 2-3 hour testing session within 5 days
- English proficient (can complete feedback form)
- Willing to provide honest feedback

**Secondary Criteria** (Desired for diversity):
- **Experience Level**:
  - 5 contractors with <2 years experience (early adopters)
  - 5 contractors with 2+ years experience (established)

- **Geography** (coverage):
  - 2-3 from NSW (Sydney, regional)
  - 2-3 from VIC (Melbourne, regional)
  - 2-3 from QLD (Brisbane, regional)
  - 1-2 from WA/SA

- **Specialization** (balance):
  - 4 water damage restoration
  - 3 fire/soot damage
  - 3 general disaster recovery

**Responsibilities**:
- Greet contractor warmly
- Explain testing purpose & confidentiality
- Guide through 5 scenarios
- Observe user interactions
- Note any issues/confusions
- Encourage honest feedback
- Collect completed feedback form
- Send follow-up thank you
